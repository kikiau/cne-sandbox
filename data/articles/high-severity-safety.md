# High-Severity Safety Issues
Source: Internal Community Policy guidance (demo)

## Covered Situations
- Weapons and armed incidents
- Sexual violence, sex work, trafficking, or involvement of minors
- Domestic or intimate-partner violence
- Drug production or distribution

## Response
These cases are treated as high-priority. Airbnb may immediately suspend accounts, cancel reservations, support law enforcement when appropriate, and conduct a full policy review. Outcomes depend on the strength of evidence and applicable policies.

# Rebooking and Refund Policy for Homes
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868

Effective Date: February 6, 2025

## If a Host Cancels Before Check-in
The guest is entitled to a full refund. Airbnb will help the guest find a similar place where appropriate. Payment is typically converted to travel credit for rebooking (or refunded to the original method on request).

## Reservation Issues
A Reservation Issue includes:
- Host cancels before check-in
- Guest cannot access the accommodation
- Accommodation is not habitable (unsafe, unclean, etc.)
- Accommodation is significantly different from what was advertised
- Special amenities do not work

Guests must report Reservation Issues within 72 hours. Airbnb may provide a full or partial refund or rebooking assistance depending on the severity and evidence.

Hosts receive no payout (or a reduced payout) when a Reservation Issue is confirmed.

# Account Restriction Appeal

If your account has been restricted and you believe this was done in error, you may submit an appeal.

## When You Can Appeal
- You believe the restriction was applied by mistake
- You have new information that was not available at the time of the action
- The restriction period has ended and you want to request reinstatement

## How to Submit an Appeal
1. Go to the Account Status page
2. Click “Appeal Restriction”
3. Clearly explain why you believe the decision should be reviewed
4. Submit supporting information if available

## Appeal Review Process
Appeals are reviewed by the Community Policy team. Most appeals receive a decision within 3–5 business days. Submitting multiple appeals for the same issue will not speed up the process.

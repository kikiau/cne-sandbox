# Escalation Guidelines

## When to Escalate to Community Policy
Escalate a case to the Community Policy team when:

- There is a potential gap in existing policy and the case is not clearly covered.
- The policy is unclear and multiple interpretations are possible.
- The policy specifically instructs escalation to Community Policy.
- The case involves high-severity safety, discrimination, or legal risk.

## Information to Include
When escalating, include:
- A clear summary of the issue
- Relevant screenshots or documentation
- Any previous outreach to the Host or Guest
- The specific policy question or gap identified

## Self-Help First
Before escalating, check whether existing Community Policies or Help Center articles already address the issue. Many common questions can be resolved through self-help.

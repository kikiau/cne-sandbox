# Community Guidelines

Our community is built on respect, safety, and constructive discussion. All users are expected to follow these guidelines.

## Expected Behavior
- Treat others with respect and courtesy
- Stay on topic and contribute meaningfully
- Respect different opinions and backgrounds

## Prohibited Behavior
- Harassment, bullying, or hate speech
- Threats of violence or harm
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Spam, scams, or misleading content
- Impersonation of others

Violations of these guidelines may result in content removal, account restrictions, or permanent suspension depending on the severity and history of violations.

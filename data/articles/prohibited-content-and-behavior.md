# Prohibited Content and Behavior

## Prohibited Activities
Members must not engage in or facilitate:

- Violence, threats, or harassment
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Fraud, scams, or misleading practices
- Illegal activity
- Content that is sexually explicit, hateful, or promotes harm

## Listing Accuracy
Hosts must provide accurate information about their listing. Significant misrepresentation (including undisclosed recording devices, unsafe conditions, or false descriptions) can result in listing removal or account restrictions.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in content removal, reservation cancellation, temporary suspension, or permanent account termination, depending on the severity and history of the Member.

# How to Report Abuse

If you see content or behavior that violates our Community Guidelines, please report it so our team can review it.

## Ways to Report
1. Click the “Report” button on the post, comment, or profile
2. Select the most relevant reason
3. Submit the report

## What Happens After You Report
- Our systems and policy team will review the report
- You will not receive a detailed update on the action taken due to privacy reasons
- Repeated or severe violations may lead to account restrictions

## False Reports
Submitting false or malicious reports may itself result in action against your account.

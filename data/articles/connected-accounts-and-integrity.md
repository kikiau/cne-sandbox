# Connected Accounts and Host Integrity
Source: Internal Community Policy guidance (demo)

## Connected / Duplicate Accounts
Users may not create multiple accounts to evade enforcement, manipulate reviews, or circumvent restrictions. Connected accounts are reviewed when patterns of abuse are detected.

## Bait-and-Switch and Misrepresentation
Hosts must accurately represent their listing. Significant differences between the advertised and actual space, or attempts to move bookings off-platform, violate Host Standards and Content Policy.

## Resource Abuse
Attempts to manipulate support systems, repeatedly file unfounded claims, or abuse AirCover/refund processes are treated as integrity issues and may result in account restrictions.

# Airbnb's Content Policy
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/546

Content includes any written, photographic, audio, video, or other content on the platform.

## Not Allowed
- Advertising unrelated businesses
- Spam or unwanted contact
- Illegal or harmful content
- Sexually explicit content
- Violent, graphic, threatening, or harassing content
- Content that violates the Nondiscrimination Policy
- Impersonation
- Content that violates intellectual property or privacy rights
- Public disclosure of private information

## Enforcement
Users are responsible for their content. Airbnb may remove content that violates this policy and may restrict or remove the associated listing or account for repeated or severe violations.

We remove content that violates our Community Guidelines or legal requirements.

## Common Reasons for Removal
- Hate speech or harassment
- Violence or threats
- Adult sexual content involving minors
- Spam or scams
- Copyright infringement
- Misinformation that may cause real-world harm

## What Happens When Content is Removed
- The content is no longer visible to other users
- The user who posted it may receive a notification
- Repeated violations can lead to account-level restrictions

## Can Removed Content Be Restored?
In some cases, yes. If you believe content was removed by mistake, you may file an appeal through the Account Status page.

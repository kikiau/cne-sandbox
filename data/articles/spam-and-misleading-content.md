# Spam and Misleading Content Policy

We do not allow spam or content that is designed to mislead users.

## What Counts as Spam
- Bulk or repetitive posting of the same content
- Artificially inflating engagement (likes, shares, followers)
- Misleading links or clickbait that does not match the content
- Coordinated inauthentic behavior

## Misleading Content
Content that deliberately presents false information as fact, especially when it may cause harm, is not allowed.

## Enforcement
Spam and misleading content may be removed, reduced in distribution, or lead to account restrictions depending on the severity and intent.

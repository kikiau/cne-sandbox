# What's Expected of Hosts
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3057

Hosts must meet basic standards to deliver a respectful, comfortable, and reliable experience.

## Key Expectations
- Reliability and accuracy (honor reservations, accurately describe the listing)
- Cleanliness, safety, and security
- Respect for guests and their property
- Clear communication
- Reasonable house rules (disclosed in the listing)
- Keep reservations and payments on Airbnb
- Comply with applicable laws and listing restrictions
- Be authorized to host

Hosts who do not meet these standards may face enforcement action.

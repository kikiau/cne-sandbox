# Content Removal Policy

We remove content that violates our Community Guidelines or legal requirements.

## Common Reasons for Removal
- Hate speech or harassment
- Violence or threats
- Adult sexual content involving minors
- Spam or scams
- Copyright infringement
- Misinformation that may cause real-world harm

## What Happens When Content is Removed
- The content is no longer visible to other users
- The user who posted it may receive a notification
- Repeated violations can lead to account-level restrictions

## Can Removed Content Be Restored?
In some cases, yes. If you believe content was removed by mistake, you may file an appeal through the Account Status page.

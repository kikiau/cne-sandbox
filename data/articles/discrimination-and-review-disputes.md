# Discrimination and Review Disputes
Source: Internal Community Policy guidance (demo)

## Discrimination
Reports of discrimination are reviewed under the Nondiscrimination Policy. This includes refusal of service, differential treatment, or discriminatory language based on protected characteristics.

## Review Disputes
Reviews may be removed only if they violate the Reviews Policy (e.g., irrelevant, retaliatory, or contain prohibited content). Disagreement with a rating or subjective opinions generally do not qualify for removal.

## Escalation
Complex discrimination cases or high-visibility review disputes involving VIP users or press attention are escalated to Community Policy for review.

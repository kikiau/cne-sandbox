# Community Disturbance and Parties
Source: Internal Community Policy guidance (demo)

## Overview
Hosts and Guests must not create or allow community disturbances, including unauthorized parties, loud noise, or gatherings that disrupt neighbors.

## Key Rules
- Listings may not be used for open-invite parties or large gatherings that violate house rules or local laws.
- Hosts are responsible for ensuring their listing is not used for disruptive events.
- Reports of shots fired, excessive noise, or neighborhood complaints are treated as high-priority safety and community issues.

## Enforcement
Confirmed disturbances can result in reservation cancellation, listing suspension, or account restrictions. Repeated issues may lead to permanent removal.

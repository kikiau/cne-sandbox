# Combating Deception, Scams, and Abuse
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3059

Guests and hosts must interact honestly on the platform. Airbnb prohibits the following:

## Scams and Misrepresentation
- Engaging in fraud or providing false, misleading, or manipulated information
- Submitting fake or altered identification or documents
- Making false reports, claims, or chargebacks
- Misrepresenting a listing, service, or experience

## Threats, Manipulation, and Abuse
- Using threats or intimidation to obtain or influence a payment, review, or other outcome
- Using abusive language or engaging in harassment
- Attempting to manipulate Airbnb’s systems, programs, accounts, or support channels

## Off-Platform Activity
Users are prohibited from communicating, booking, paying, or sharing personal contact information outside of the platform (with limited exceptions).

## Enforcement
Violations can result in warnings, listing suspension, or account removal.

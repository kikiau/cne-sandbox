# Safety and Security Policy

## Overview
Airbnb requires that Hosts and Guests prioritize the safety and security of everyone involved in a stay or experience.

## Recording Devices
Hosts must disclose any recording devices (such as security cameras or audio recording equipment) that are present in or around the listing. 

- Recording devices are not permitted inside private spaces such as bedrooms, bathrooms, or sleeping areas.
- Any recording device must be clearly disclosed in the listing description before booking.
- Devices that are not powered on or not functioning still need to be disclosed if they are present.

If a Guest reports an undisclosed or improperly placed recording device, the case is reviewed under the Safety and Security Policy. Depending on the circumstances, Airbnb may cancel the reservation, restrict the Host’s account, or take other enforcement action.

## Other Safety Expectations
- Hosts must not endanger Guests.
- Guests must not engage in behavior that threatens the safety of the Host or others.
- Reports of weapons, threats, or physical harm are treated as high-priority safety issues.

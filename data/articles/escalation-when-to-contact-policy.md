# When to Contact the Community Policy Team

Most issues can be handled through normal reporting tools. You should only escalate to the Community Policy team in specific situations.

## Appropriate Cases for Escalation
- You have already reported content and believe it was incorrectly left up
- You are appealing an account restriction and need further review
- There is a potential legal or safety issue that requires urgent attention
- You are a trusted reporter or partner with additional context

## What to Include in an Escalation
- Clear description of the issue
- Links to the relevant content or account
- Previous report or appeal reference numbers (if any)
- Why you believe normal channels were not sufficient

Escalations that do not meet these criteria may be closed without action.

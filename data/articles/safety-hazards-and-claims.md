# Listing Safety Hazards and Claims
Source: Internal Community Policy guidance (demo)

## Overview
Listings must be safe and free from serious health or safety hazards. Significant issues can trigger AirCover claims or high-liability incident (HLI) reviews.

## Examples of Covered Issues
- Gas leaks, fire hazards, or exposed wiring
- Structural problems that create fall risks
- Serious cleanliness or sanitation issues that make the space uninhabitable
- Failure to disclose known hazards

## Process
Guests should report safety issues promptly. Airbnb may cancel the reservation, assist with rebooking, and review the Host for policy violations. Claims are evaluated under AirCover and related policies.

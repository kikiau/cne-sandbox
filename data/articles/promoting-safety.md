# Promoting the Safety of Our Community
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3063

Safety is a top priority. When safety incidents occur, Airbnb may provide support to impacted users, cancel reservations, rebook guests, and take action against users who violate policies (including account removal). Airbnb may also support law enforcement investigations.

## How to Report a Safety Issue
- In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
- Otherwise, contact Airbnb’s 24-hour safety line.
- For issues in the surrounding community, contact Neighborhood Support.

## Related Policies
Airbnb has policies addressing:
- Illegal activities, physical violence, sexual misconduct, and threats
- Disruptive gatherings
- Listings with safety hazards
- Dangerous animals
- Unsafe or illegal handling of weapons

# Nondiscrimination Policy

## Core Principle
Airbnb prohibits discrimination based on race, color, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, or disability.

## Host Requirements
Hosts may not:
- Refuse to host based on a protected characteristic
- Impose different terms, prices, or rules based on a protected characteristic
- Make discriminatory statements in listings or messages

Limited exceptions exist (for example, certain safety or legal requirements), but these must be applied carefully and consistently.

## Guest Expectations
Guests are also expected to treat Hosts and others with respect and without discrimination.

## Enforcement
Confirmed violations of the Nondiscrimination Policy can result in account suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

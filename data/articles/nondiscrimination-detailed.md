# Nondiscrimination Policy
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2867

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Airbnb’s Nondiscrimination Policy applies to all listings on its platform—homes, services, and experiences.


## Core Principle
Airbnb prohibits discrimination based on race, color, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, or disability.

## Key Rules
- Users may not use discriminatory language, slurs, or hateful speech.
- Users may not display hateful symbols, images, or objects.
- Hosts may not refuse service or impose different terms based on a protected characteristic.
- Hosts may not decide for guests that a listing is unsuitable based on age, disability, or gender identity (with limited legal exceptions).
- Hosts must respect guests’ expressed gender identity.

## Enforcement
Violations can result in warnings, listing suspension, or account removal. Guests who report discrimination may receive support under the Open Doors Policy.

## Guest Expectations
Guests are also expected to treat Hosts and others with respect and without discrimination.
